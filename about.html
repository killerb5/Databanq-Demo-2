About DataBanq

Govern the agents first. Make enterprise AI defensible.

DataBanq was built for compliance, legal risk, and engineering leaders who need proof of identity, authorization, and audit integrity as AI agents enter regulated workflows.

Why we exist

Most AI governance platforms were built for dashboards, model inventories, or generic policy tracking. None were built for autonomous agents acting across systems with real permissions and real consequences.

DataBanq focuses on the urgent gap: agent identity, A2A trust, blast radius limiting, model provenance, and regulator-ready evidence.

Our positioning is simple: Govern the Agents. Then Govern Everything.

What we believe

  • Identity comes before compliance reporting.
  • Authorization must be enforced in real time, not documented after the fact.
  • Evidence should be cryptographically provable and exportable on demand.
  • Fintech and healthtech will feel the first enforcement pressure.

Primary buyer

Chief compliance officers, VP Legal Risk leaders, CTOs, and Heads of AI at organizations with 150–1,500 employees operating under audit and regulatory pressure.

Design partner cohort

We are selectively onboarding a small 2026 cohort from fintech and healthtech to shape the roadmap around real-world governance requirements.
